FAQ
Frequently asked questions

    
What does Flo Creative do?
Flo Creative works in two disciplines: exhibition design & build, and interior design for retail, F&B and pop-ups. Every project is shaped around the client’s brand, from concept to handover. We are based in Dubai and work across the UAE, KSA and wider MENA.
Do you deliver stands outside the UAE?
Yes. The EOE stand for Eurospine 2026 in Sweden was designed in Dubai and delivered overseas. The PL Solutions stand in Riyadh was built through our sister company in KSA.
Who builds the stand?
We lead concept, 3D renders, technical drawings and project management. Build and installation are delivered by our production partners, and the stand is handed over to match the approved design on the agreed date.
What do you need to prepare a proposal?
Event name, dates and stand size, plus your objectives. The proposal form on this page covers the essentials.
What kind of interiors do you design?
Retail, F&B, pop-ups and showrooms: fit-out, space planning, FF&E, specifications, technical drawings and renders.
